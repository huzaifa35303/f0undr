## 📖 Overview

**The Art of Mental Mastery** is a premium, research-grounded digital handbook designed to give learners, leaders, and curious minds superpowers in accelerated learning, human behavior decoding, deception detection, and rapid decision-making. 

Styled in the same elegant editorial standard as the acclaimed **f0undr Computer E-Book**, this interactive platform features deep pedagogical text, custom high-resolution architectural SVG diagrams, interactive self-assessment quizzes, a real-time modal search engine (`Ctrl + K`), auto-saving student workbooks, and a 3D flashcard study suite.

## 🌟 Key Features

- **6 Comprehensive Units**:
  1. *Accelerated Learning & Neuroplasticity* (Synaptic LTP, Feynman Technique, Ultralearning, Speed Reading, Flow State).
  2. *Memory Architecture & Mnemonics* (Sherlockian Method of Loci, Major System, PAO System, Sensory Anchoring).
  3. *Non-Verbal Mind Reading & Body Language* (Paul Ekman's 7 Microexpressions, FBI Limbic Honesty Inversion, Ocular Cues).
  4. *Behavioral Profiling & Deception Detection* (Cognitive Load, SCAN Statement Analysis, Linguistic Distancing, Cold Reading).
  5. *Persuasion, Influence & Dark Psychology Defense* (Cialdini's 7 Levers, Anchoring, Framing, Gray Rock Defense).
  6. *Mental Models & Rapid Decisions (Superthinking)* (First Principles, Inversion, Second-Order Logic, Razors, Stoic Control).
- **Interactive Student Lab Workbooks**: Practice prompts auto-saved to browser `localStorage` with a 1-click Markdown export.
- **Interactive Self-Assessment Quizzes**: 30 MCQs across Units 1–6 with live scoring and explanations.
- **3D Interactive Flashcards**: 30+ cards with categories, flip animation, and Mastered/Review tracking.
- **In-Book Live Search Engine**: Instant search dialog accessible via `Ctrl + K` or header icon with category filter chips.
- **3 Visual Reader Themes**: Warm Antique Parchment, Midnight Emerald Dark Mode, and Clean Editorial White.
- **Vector Architectural Diagrams**: Inline SVG diagrams for LTP plasticity, Ebbinghaus forgetting curve, Mind Palace floorplan, FACS microexpressions, FBI body language pyramid, and Mental Models.
- **1-Click Print & PDF Generation**: Clean print media CSS optimized for physical reading.
